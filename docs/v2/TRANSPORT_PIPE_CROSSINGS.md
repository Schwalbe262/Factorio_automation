# Underground belts beneath existing pipes

`plan_connection` may cross an ordinary existing `pipe` with a normal
underground-belt pair. Its native survey first matches the pipe to an observed
unit and exact position in the bound world, verifies ownership and surface, and
records its actual reciprocal fluid peers. Unverified, replaced, foreign,
prospective and underground pipes remain hard obstacles. Ore, water, mining
reservations and all prospective non-belt footprints retain their checks.

Whole non-mining production PCB reservations and shared-bus future bands are
also hard, including the entire buried path. A pipe inside another producer's
future machine area cannot make that area crossable. The first outside cell of
a registered port's bounded continuation stays clear for its future connection.
Only a unique exact registered item output with a fresh owned native belt may
open its empty straight outward apron, through the first outside cell within
four steps. A matching declared/owned input may use its similarly bounded entry.
These cells receive ordinary whole-footprint preflight, native identity and item
purity checks; the rest of the producer reservation remains hard. Existing
mining-core and mining-exit policies are unchanged.
The apron shares the route's fresh native survey and final whole-corridor
preflight. Every apron point must remain in the compiled physical entities,
avoiding a duplicate preliminary query while retaining the same checks.

`compile_crossings(..., verified_pipes=...)` buries only the crossing interior.
Both mouths must occupy clear cells with a straight approach and exit. Catalog
span limits, existing underground partner checks and all belt merge checks still
apply. Pipe direction does not impose a belt junction: the systems use separate
native transport layers. The plan builds no pipe and never rotates, mines,
removes or inserts fluid into the existing network.

Each resulting `underground_pairs` entry carries `preserved_pipes` witnesses.
Controllers that merge route pairs must preserve these entries and call
`surface_pipe_proof(game, obs, plan)` before returning construction actions and
again during commissioning proof. It returns a report with an explicit boolean
`ok`, succeeds without I/O for plans without pipe crossings, and otherwise checks
the exact original units, force, world, positions and reciprocal native peer
graph. Changing a pipe or its peers blocks; the witness never adopts a new unit.
Engine production uses both gates while retaining the ordinary native belt pair
proof.

Focused tests execute the flattened native survey and proof scripts. They cover
valid crossings, stale units, foreign ownership, resource/water collisions,
additional obstacles, peer replacement, one-way peers, added/missing peers,
straight-mouth/span restrictions, prospective footprint keepouts and a guard
that blocks the engine builder when preservation proof fails.

Native pipe preservation checks passed against the real crude/gas network.
Complete engine preparation must also pass the production reservation and
ordinary corridor gates; pipe crossing permission alone is insufficient.
Normal paid construction and native underground partner proof belong to the
executor.
