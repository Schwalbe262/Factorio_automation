# One concentrated projectile wave

`NestSweep(game,bootstrap,builder,catalog,perimeter, *, ammo_source=None)` exposes `prepare(obs)`,
`step(obs)` and `proof(obs)`. It owns `nest-sweep.json`; old paused assault history
is preserved. A compatible unlaunched prior intent can supply exact target IDs.
Otherwise it selects a close stationary cluster from the bounded charted native
survey. The checkpoint records the source of that intent explicitly.

This operation funds and commits **one ten-gun wave**. It never advances through
successively constructed bastions. All ten normally paid guns receive20 full
normal piercing magazines in the same native tick, after every footprint,
stock, identity, known-worm and whole-wave guard passes. Existing50 red magazines
count toward the200 total; no conversion or item creation shortcut is used.
Walls are optional afterward and cannot delay the initial firing wave.

Missing ammunition comes from the commissioned permanent piercing-ammunition
factory. The sweep issues no new red or yellow magazine craft actions and does
not acquire raw ingredients as a handcraft fallback. Its optional trusted
`ammo_source` exposes `commissioned` and `ensure_full_magazines(obs, count)`.
The stock target is200; each actual `take_factory_magazines` transfer is at most50.
An absent or unfinished factory returns a waiting result naming the
`piercing_ammo` capability and the actual held/missing full stock. Empty output
waits for ordinary assembler production rather than fabricating a surplus rate.

Already paid inventory and an already queued native batch remain usable. Legacy
paired successful `started` receipts reconcile once into their original history;
queue-busy receipts charge zero and uncertain outcomes still block replay. An
active paid queue completes normally before launching. Existing craft counters
are neither reset nor reinterpreted as factory output. Once stock is available,
the unchanged native idle/full-stock launch guard applies.

`factory_magazine_action(game, obs, count, source_slots)` in
`factory_ammo_transfer.py` emits the typed collection action.
`execute_take_factory_magazines(game, action)` independently reloads the fixed
`piercing-ammo.json` producer, its exact verified plan and bound output chest,
the PCB's `piercing_ammo` block, and the pinned catalog fingerprint. Native
guards then recheck world, actor, force, surface, exact chest unit/position,
selected slots and actual inventory capacity. Only full normal red magazines
move; source and destination partial magazines retain their remaining rounds.
Every transfer conserves selected item counts and rounds. The original yellow
ammo helper and its shared guard-string interface remain unchanged.

This is assisted logistics between normally stocked owned inventories, not an
instant craft or item grant. Factory production and its finite buffers have their
own ordinary material, circuit and commissioning proofs; nameplate output alone
does not establish a sustained supply rate.

Full stock is checked before the native10800tick combat clock starts. Setup can
take longer. The launch helper independently requires ten plain normal guns,
200 full normal red magazines and an idle crafting queue for a new operation.
After stocking, the whole formation and rear bindings are freshly prepared.
The actor remains at least48tiles from all targets and never travels forward.

Read-only setup geometry may use actual healthy armed rear posts with at least
one full magazine. Launch requires two fresh exact half-health-or-better rear
anchors with at least five full normal magazines. Front guns need no direct
two-base-gun coverage. Instead, every planned gun has two planned neighbors;
the support graph stays connected to both rear anchors after any one planned
gun is removed. Every stationary target is within range of at least two guns.
The native macro recomputes that geometry and rejects new covering worms.

Candidate generation uses the target envelope and two concurrent approach chains,
at most256 positions and three whole-formation alternatives. Normal ore, mining
growth, registered PCB/source approaches and immutable enemy collisions remain
hard constraints. Mobile enemies entering a previously clear footprint cause a
finite alternative or a wait, never deletion. Unaccepted offline factory route
previews are not treated as registered reservations.

The trusted owner is `nest_sweep`, with mode`committed_projectile_wave_v2` and a
private stage registry. `deployment_wave` and the three native `SWEEP_*_LUA`
exports authorize the existing guarded group executor. After aggregate preflight,
`SWEEP_BEGIN_WAVE_LUA` locks exactly one native group ID before first payment.
Partial or uncertain groups cannot be replayed as a new wave. No offensive post
is added to global Watch, and no lost gun is automatically replaced or fed.

The typed `start_nest_sweep` action is handled by `execute_start_sweep`. It reuses
the reviewed native physical-projectile death ledger with a distinct operation
ID. Unknown handlers are preserved and block admission. Missing owned listeners
can be restored without resetting the deadline. Ordinary worm-first UI priorities
keep other targets enabled. No enemy damage, health, destroy or research setter
is present. A third gun loss does not prematurely cancel a committed wave.

Completion requires all original IDs to be absent and have exact native friendly
gun physical-projectile death receipts. Losses, surviving guns and the single
group ID are reported. All guns lost, prepaid rounds exhausted or180 game seconds
end the operation without another wave or indefinite forward rebuilding.
