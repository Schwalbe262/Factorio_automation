# Additive sulfur water header

`WaterDistribution(game,builder,catalog,perimeter,*,water=None)` exposes
`prepare(obs)`, `step(obs)` and `proof(obs)`. The public capability is intended
to be `water_distribution`; parent integration owns CLI/job wiring. There are
no new native action types: the normal builder pays for clearance, pipes and
underground mouths. Preparation and proofs make no gameplay changes.

The controller requires the existing verified `water-gathering.json`, identical
PCB producer plan/bindings and exact original published `sources.water`. It
selects the middle owned pipe as an explicitly authorized producer branch,
then plans a32-tile north header and three empty continuation cells. The old
pump, three pipes, plan, original east outlet and registry descriptor remain
unchanged. The new narrow corridor reserves material flow north; its initial
one-cell producer apron is recorded separately. No mining/resource exemptions
are applied to pipes or buried spans.

Native survey records every preserved belt identity/direction and reciprocal
foreign pipe graph. Nearby crossing bands may share one normally paid straight
underground pair within the installed native span. Current-world planning
identified coal belt7485 and crude pipe6885; the canonical orientations derive
from the existing pipe compiler, rather than guessed directions. Terrain,
resources, mining growth, foreign PCB reservations and unbuilt external routes
remain hard. New unrelated pipes or buried partners block construction.

The header is accepted only after a full prospective sulfur plant/feed/power/
defense plan passes. This preview uses an explicit `ProspectiveWaterSource`
whose zero unit sentinel means unbuilt planning only. It cannot be executed as
a ChemicalProduction checkpoint or published as a source. Actual publication
of `fluid-sources.json` / `sources.sulfur-water` requires all normally built
units, actual water at the new outlet, reciprocal surface/underground edges,
the original intake on the same current segment, separate crossed foreign
networks, installed pipeline extent and armed coverage. Source capacity is
shared with the old outlet; adding a branch does not multiply supply.

## Two fluid networks in one chemical consumer

The first planned fluid route can otherwise wall off the second before either is built.
`joint_fluid_routing.py` accepts only a typed context generated by the owning
chemical planner after a prior same-core route succeeds. Every proposed normal
pipe and its halo must have been clear in that route's independent native
survey and remain clear in the water route's native, resource, PCB, future and
core hard sets. Live native assets are never made soft by this mechanism.

Permitted halo cells must be used only inside one straight, bounded underground
crossing of exactly one declared same-consumer prospective pipe. Surface use,
mouths in the halo, changed prior/core identity, multiple crossings and any
independent obstruction fail closed. Both networks retain full prospective
isolation and native reciprocal-peer proof. Once the crossed gas pipe exists,
its exact saved binding and fluid type are required; a replacement or unknown
unit blocks further construction and proof.

Both water-under-gas and gas-under-water use identical guards. Catalog iteration
order is preserved. The controller does not force an extra gas detour or reverse
accepted pipe geometry merely to select one crossing direction.
